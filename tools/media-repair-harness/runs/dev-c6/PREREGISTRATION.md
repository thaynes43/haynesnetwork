## 3. Retry, repair, timeout and abort rules (the harness defaults, frozen)

* **Repair:** a reply that is not one valid JSON object, or fails the strict validator (for a decision: the frozen decision-v3 validator), gets exactly ONE generic repair prompt (the validator error plus a fixed hint per failure mode; under `turn-v3` no audit hint and, for a decision-v3 shape error, the `decision_v3_shape` hint). If the repaired reply is also invalid the decision is FORCED to `insufficient_evidence` / `escalate` (no target) and recorded `forced`. First-attempt validity is never repaired after the fact.
* **No whole-case rerun, ever, and no rerun of any pass.** A failed model call (CLI error or timeout, a Codex call with no agent message or a plan or rate limit, an Ollama transport error or call timeout) is recorded with its receipt (tokens and cost when the failure reports them), the case ends as a forced escalate, and the run moves on. A run whose calls fail repeatedly (for example a plan quota wall) is reported as it is, with the adapter-error count next to every table; it is not repeated.
* **Before the first case only:** a failure that is not a model result and happens before any case starts (a CLI not found, a preflight error) leaves its directory in place; for Haiku or Astra the pass may be started ONCE more with the same registered command under the run id with suffix `-r2`, and both attempts are reported. For qwen a busy Ollama or a digest mismatch skips and a load failure aborts (section 2); neither is repeated.
* **Aborts** (the run stops, exit 3, reason in the manifest): the qwen skip and abort conditions above, the PSI/restart guard trip and the qwen wall cap. Haiku and Astra have no cap and no abort beyond a preflight failure.
* **Started-aborted and never-started, never silent.** Every planned case ends in exactly one of `records_written`, `started_aborted` (persisted in `started-aborted/<case>.json` with every turn, tool call, usage figure and the abort reason) and `never_started`. **Scoring treatment, registered: started-aborted cases are NOT scored; they are listed unscored.** The unchanged scorer reads only `<case>.json` records, so they appear in its `missing` list and G9 crash list. **Each gate keeps score.py's own denominator; G7 is measured over the planned cases.** Recorded, started-aborted and never-started counts are reported next to every gate, and a partial run is labelled PARTIAL in every table.
* **Context overflow (qwen, 8192 tokens), the context-subset rule:** Ollama truncates an over-long prompt silently and nothing is done to prevent it (`num_ctx` 8192, the system prompt, the cases and the page budget are frozen). Each turn is flagged by the adapter from the REPORTED token counts (`prompt_at_context_limit`, `context_full_after_generation`) and, separately, by the 3-characters-per-token ESTIMATE (`estimated_prompt_exceeds_context`, pessimistic, not evidence of truncation). The report states the number of turns and cases with a reported-count flag next to every qwen table, scores all recorded cases as recorded, and adds TWO subset rows (offline `score.py --only` rescores, labelled as subsets, never as the whole suite): (a) cases with no reported-count flag, and (b) cases with neither a reported-count flag nor the estimate flag. Cases the estimate flags but the reported counts do not are labelled **possibly truncated**. Registered expectation from the offline counts (section 1): no first message reaches the estimate, and replayed dev-c5 call sequences reach it in 6 of 73 cases, so subset (b) may be several cases smaller than the whole set. For every flagged qwen turn the report also gives the ratio of the reported to the estimated prompt tokens (Amendment 1). Nothing is rerun. The same two rows are not computed for the hosted models (no context limit applies).
* **Hosted models:** the response model id of every record is checked against the request; any difference is reported in the run table.

## 6. Benchmark-passability report (Astra; neutral evidence for the evaluator; no label change is proposed)

Computed by `scripts/passability.py` (sha256 in section 1; policy `passability-1`; every category is reachable in `tests/test_passability.py`) from the copied Astra records, their restored copies, the suite, the unchanged score.py's per-case facts and the frozen rubric v3.1 checker (`experimental/contract_v3/prereq.py`, unchanged). Offline: no model call; the only tool work is the unchanged replay tools serving the recorded fixtures.
**Rows.** (a) Every case whose guarded decision score.py marks not `correct` or `unsafe_write`; (b) every write, guarded or restored, whose audit v3 reports a prerequisite that is not met (unmet or unknown), even when score.py marks it correct (Astra 22 item 6). score.py's verdicts (guarded and restored) are kept beside every row, unchanged.
**Columns.** Case id; the label (truth class, accepted classes, supported actions, as score.py reads them); the decision, guarded and restored (class / action, and source and target for a write); the calls made; the audit v3 prerequisites that are not met (class / action, diagnosis, action and reference roles); the category and its basis; for a model-decision row, S (the state of every prerequisite of every accepted class with every supported action it can take, on what the model was shown), the fidelity check and the witness search; and, as an INDEPENDENT disclosed annotation on every row, membership of the frozen 6-case label-tension list with its frozen reason (it never decides the category).
**Fidelity** (Astra 22 item 4): every reference that decides a met prerequisite on S (observation, finding, field, value) is looked up in the transcript ACTUALLY SENT (the last request of the case, which holds the whole conversation) before a model error is assigned.
**Frozen exploration policy `passability-1`** (Astra 22 item 5), for what is obtainable: (1) the advertised operations: for every offered tool whose capability is not `available: false`, in `tools_available` order: ffprobe, epub_inspect, audiobook_inspect and arr_lookup `{}`; decode_sample `{"at": o}` for every advertised offset, then `{"full": true}` when the full decode is advertised as recorded, on request or a bounded run; audio_sample `{"at": o, "stream": 0}` for every advertised offset; frame_sample and ocr `{"at": o}` for every advertised offset or, when the offsets are `any`, the start of every recorded reading range and 0; a tool with no advertisement `{}` (windowed tools `{"at": 0}`); (2) the newly revealed candidates: every `available` link of the v3 open list over the case evidence plus all results served so far and over the case evidence plus each single served result, and ocr `{"frame": name}` for every frame a served frame_sample returned, for up to 3 rounds; a candidate equivalent to case evidence and an unavailable result are excluded; (3) a **witness**: the smallest set of distinct served candidates (iterative deepening, pool order) whose results, with the case evidence, meet every prerequisite of some accepted pair (insufficient_evidence: no other class and not healthy established; reference roles depend on a written decision and are assessed for written decisions only), whose cost (calls plus every extra page of the first message and of each witness result, counted by the real v3 session) is at most **6**; at most 5,000 checker evaluations per row, a search that reaches it is reported `truncated`. **Coverage limits, reported with every row:** replay only (no live collector); offsets outside the advertisement and frames other than those returned are not explored; stream arguments other than 0 are not explored; sets larger than six calls are not searched; free-text identity and M9 stay `unknown` to the checker.
**Category, the first that applies:**
1. **non-model failure**: the recorded decision was forced (an adapter error, invalid output after the one repair, a harness error): not a model decision;
2. **guard change**: the model's own (restored) decision is score-correct and not unsafe, and the guard's escalation is what score.py marks wrong (attributed to the guard, separately);
3. **score-correct write with unmet or unknown prerequisites**: the row is present only through rule (b);
4. **model error**: the label is met on S and the fidelity check passes;
5. **not obtained (unfetched page)**: met on S, but a deciding reference is absent from the sent transcript and a message of the case had pages that were not fetched;
6. **presentation problem**: met on S, but a deciding reference is absent from the sent transcript otherwise;
7. **obtainable, not obtained**: not met on S, and a witness of cost at most 6 exists under `passability-1` (an uncalled link alone never makes a model error, and this is not counted as one);
8. **undetermined (hand judgement)**: not met on S, no witness, and a relevant prerequisite is `unknown` to the checker;
9. **evidence gap**: not met on S and no witness under `passability-1`: the case does not offer what the contract requires for its label (with the coverage limits and, when it happened, the truncation).
The script makes no hand judgement. Any judgement made afterwards (an `unknown` prerequisite, a row to re-read) is recorded in the report with its reason and marked, beside the computed category, which is never overwritten. The section ends with the count per category and the cross-reference: which of the 6 tension cases Astra got right and which wrong.
