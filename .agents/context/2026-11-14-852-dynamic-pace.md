## Necessary recovery arithmetic for story 823 (estimates)

Recomputed on 2026-11-14 with
`node --experimental-strip-types .agents/context/2026-11-14-852-projection.mjs`.
Every future quantity in this section is an **estimate**, using the historical inputs above,
constant intake 329.7669471492291 GB/day and yield 0.8723303941670187. These are necessary
throughput/supply bounds, not forecasts or sufficient settings. Decimal TB; round only the display.
The initial gap is targetFree minus initialFree. Required freed bytes = initial gap + intake × days.
The open batch contributes its expected yield once on day 7; new deletions start on day 14 and
repeat every 7 days. Future deletions = floor(deadline / 7) − 1. New proposed supply =
(required freed bytes − open batch bytes × yield) / yield; average batch = new supply / future deletions.
