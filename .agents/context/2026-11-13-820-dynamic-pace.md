## Projection

This is a projection, not a stored forecast. The figures are the adversarial review's measurement of 2026-11-13. TB is 1e12 bytes. The first draft's week table is withdrawn: it counted the open batch both inside the eligible pool and again as a separate deletion.

Rule for the central figures, checked in this correction on 2026-11-13 with `python3` integer arithmetic. It is not a new live read.

- Eligible pool not yet posted = 434 − 50 titles = 384, and 7,677,954,075,421 − 1,881,676,268,710 = 5,796,277,806,711 bytes = 5.796 TB.
- Deleted in 60 days = yield 0.880520 × (5,796,277,806,711 + 1,881,676,268,710) = 6,760,592,122,490 bytes = 6.761 TB. The open batch is in that sum once.
- Required deletion over 60 days stays the first projection's 63.180 TB (gap 43.394 TB plus 19.786 TB of intake). This correction did not remeasure it. The review used it.
- Shortfall = 63.180 − 6.761 = 56.419 TB.
- Day-60 free space is the review's stated 76.07 TB. This correction did not re-simulate the path that produces it. Used percent = 1 − 76.07 / 529.959 = 85.65% at two decimals. 529.959 TB is the review's rounding of the measured total 529,958,515,245,056 bytes.
- Ceiling 3,591,025,375,575 bytes = 3.591 TB. Remainder = 5,796,277,806,711 − 3,591,025,375,575 = 2,205,252,431,136 bytes = 2.205 TB. Day 7 (2026-11-20) posts 3.591 TB. Day 14 (2026-11-27) posts the last 2.205 TB. Those bytes delete on 2026-12-04. The pool is used up by the batch posted on 2026-11-27.

HaynesTower does not return to 75 percent within 60 days. The array is at 85.65 percent at day 60.

With today's pool the pace deletes 0.04 TB more by day 60 than today's cap. The review's comparison is today's fixed cap at 6.72 TB and Dynamic Pace at 6.76 TB (6.76 − 6.72 = 0.04). The precise pace total is 6.761 TB. The 6.72 TB cap figure is the review's; this correction did not recompute that cap path.

## Open questions

- Q-07. The +8.7 TB rise is not intake and not Trash. The review timed 8,851 GB between 14:00 and 16:00 UTC on 2026-11-11, with no app deletion in that window. The forecast sets that step aside. Recommendation: leave it that way.
- Q-08. The save rate is not stationary (last batch about 34% of bytes, 90-day rate 0.119480). Recommendation: keep the 90-day rate.
- Q-09. Whether the rules refill the pool. The projection assumes they do not. The review estimated refill adds about 1.2 TB, shortfall about 55.2 TB.
- Q-10. Whether skips (5.0% of movie proposed bytes) should reduce the yield. Recommendation: no.
- Q-11. Whether the floor should be the smallest 50-item total (800,535,856,306) instead of the 19-item total. Recommendation: keep the smaller floor.
- Q-12 (review Q-R01). Under target, what is the steady pace? The code asks for the same amount at any level under the target, and at a target of 100 percent. Recommendation: nothing until the trend would cross the target inside the horizon, then only what holds the target.
- Q-13 (review Q-R02). Is the horizon a deadline or a time constant? Recommendation: keep the rolling rule, call it a time constant, and set it to 21 to 30 days.
- Q-14 (review Q-R03). What is the largest batch in one 7-day window, in bytes and in titles? Recommendation: keep 3.59 TB until story 823, add a title cap, and decide both against the pool that exists then.
- Q-15 (review Q-R04). Does TV keep its 5-title cap while the pace is on? Recommendation: yes, until story 835 decides.

These stay open for the owner. The coordinator's intake (story 820, comment 8175) states the same recommendations for Q-12 and Q-15, a 30-day horizon for Q-13, and leaves Q-14 to story 823.
